# gdipath clauses (spec/README.md): <id> <status> | <source> | <what it says>
GDI.PATH.BEGIN doc | BeginPath | opens a path bracket; the DC's previous path is discarded
GDI.PATH.BEGINAGAIN open | BeginPath | BeginPath while a bracket is open (what happens to the points so far)
GDI.PATH.END doc | EndPath | closes the bracket and selects the path into the DC; fails (0) when no bracket is open
GDI.PATH.ABORT doc | AbortPath | closes and discards any path; succeeds with none
GDI.PATH.CLOSE doc | CloseFigure | closes the open figure: its last point gets PT_CLOSEFIGURE; fails outside a bracket
GDI.PATH.CLOSEEMPTY open | CloseFigure | CloseFigure in a bracket with no figure
GDI.PATH.RECORD doc | Paths (the functions that define a path) | in a bracket, LineTo, MoveToEx, Polyline, PolylineTo, Polygon, Rectangle, ... add their points instead of drawing; a line starts a figure (PT_MOVETO) at the current position
GDI.PATH.MOVETO assumed | GetPath | MoveToEx starts a new figure (a PT_MOVETO); one that no line follows is replaced by the next
GDI.PATH.RECT assumed | Rectangle | a rectangle in a path: four points from the corner (right, top) counterclockwise, or (right, bottom) clockwise (SetArcDirection), the right and bottom edges excluded (r - 1, b - 1), the figure closed
GDI.PATH.CURVE open | Ellipse, Arc, PolyBezier, FlattenPath, ... | the Bézier points of curves in a path, and the lines FlattenPath makes of them
GDI.PATH.GET doc | GetPath | the closed path's points and their types (PT_MOVETO 6, PT_LINETO 2, PT_BEZIERTO 4, | PT_CLOSEFIGURE 1); -1 when the DC has no closed path
GDI.PATH.FLATTEN doc | FlattenPath | curves become lines; a path of lines stays as it is
GDI.PATH.STROKE doc | StrokePath | draws the path's lines with the pen (as LineTo, the last point of an open figure not drawn; a closed figure's closing line) and discards the path
GDI.PATH.NOPATH doc | FillPath, StrokeAndFillPath, SelectClipPath, PathToRegion | fail (0, no region) when the DC has no closed path
GDI.PATH.FILL open | FillPath, StrokeAndFillPath, SelectClipPath, PathToRegion | which pixels a path's interior holds
GDI.PATH.WIDEN open | WidenPath | the outline a geometric pen makes of the path
GDI.PATH.MITER open | SetMiterLimit | the limits taken, and the old value
