GDI.PATH.BEGIN doc | BeginPath | opens a path bracket; the DC's previous path is discarded
GDI.PATH.BEGINAGAIN measured | golden path order-2 | BeginPath in an open bracket drops the points so far and begins an empty path (the current position stays)
GDI.PATH.END doc | EndPath | closes the bracket and selects the path into the DC; fails (0) when no bracket is open
GDI.PATH.ABORT doc | AbortPath | closes and discards any path; succeeds with none
GDI.PATH.CLOSE doc | CloseFigure | closes the open figure: its last point gets PT_CLOSEFIGURE; fails outside a bracket
GDI.PATH.CLOSEEMPTY measured | golden path order-4 | CloseFigure with no figure open (none yet, a lone MoveToEx, or the last one closed) succeeds and changes nothing
GDI.PATH.RECORD doc | Paths (the functions that define a path) | in a bracket, LineTo, MoveToEx, Polyline, PolylineTo, Polygon, Rectangle, ... add their points instead of drawing; a line starts a figure (PT_MOVETO) at the current position
GDI.PATH.MOVETO assumed | GetPath | MoveToEx starts a new figure (a PT_MOVETO); one that no line follows is replaced by the next
GDI.PATH.RECT assumed | Rectangle | a rectangle in a path: four points from the corner (right, top) counterclockwise, or (right, bottom) clockwise (SetArcDirection), the right and bottom edges excluded (r - 1, b - 1), the figure closed
GDI.PATH.CURVE open | RoundRect, Arc, ArcTo, Pie, Chord | RoundRect's figure with an empty box and a corner 0 (curve-33 fills nothing) and with a negative corner, Ellipse's figure for an empty box (curve-57, curve-63 draw nothing but the path is not empty), ArcTo, Pie and Chord of an empty box, arcs under GM_ADVANCED or a transform, and curves too large for the 32-bit flattener (a control point 1024 pixels or more from the bound box's corner: GDI's 64-bit flattener; stroked curves are GDI.LINE.GIQ, measured)
GDI.PATH.GET doc | GetPath | the closed path's points and their types (PT_MOVETO 6, PT_LINETO 2, PT_BEZIERTO 4, | PT_CLOSEFIGURE 1); -1 when the DC has no closed path
GDI.PATH.FLATTEN doc | FlattenPath | curves become lines; a path of lines stays as it is
GDI.PATH.STROKE doc | StrokePath | draws the path's lines with the pen (as LineTo, the last point of an open figure not drawn; a closed figure's closing line) and discards the path
GDI.PATH.NOPATH doc | FillPath, StrokeAndFillPath, SelectClipPath, PathToRegion | fail (0, no region) when the DC has no closed path
GDI.PATH.STROKEFILLFIX measured | StrokeAndFillPath; golden path curve-* shape-* | a path whose flattened points fall between pixels: after the fill, each figure's closed outline in 28.4 by GDI.LINE.GIQ

GDI.PATH.EMPTYFILL measured | golden path order-3 | FillPath, StrokeAndFillPath, SelectClipPath, PathToRegion of an empty closed path fail (0, no region) and discard the path
GDI.PATH.WIDEN open | WidenPath | the outline a geometric pen makes of the path
GDI.PATH.MITER measured | golden path miter | SetMiterLimit: a limit below 1 fails without writing the old value; otherwise returns the old limit (10 at first) and keeps the new one
GDI.PATH.ENDMOVE measured | golden path order-5 | EndPath drops a PT_MOVETO that ends the path (a MoveToEx after the last figure)
GDI.PATH.POLYSHORT assumed | Polyline, Polygon | in a path, fewer than two points fail as outside one, recording nothing
GDI.PATH.BEZIER measured | golden path shape-13-* shape-14-* curve-* | PolyBezier in a path begins a figure at its first point (replacing a lone MoveToEx), PolyBezierTo continues from the current position; the points are kept as given, each PT_BEZIERTO
GDI.PATH.ARC measured | Arc, ArcTo, Pie, Chord in a path; clean-room note on win32kfull (arcs); golden path curve-* arcpath-* arcflat-* (GetPath, FlattenPath and PathToRegion, both arches) | in float32, in the order given: the box ordered, its top and bottom swapped with AD_CLOCKWISE, in 28.4 one pixel in on the right and bottom (Ellipse's box: an empty one records nothing); each radial's parametric angle on the logical box's ellipse, (x - (l + r) / 2) / ((r - l) / 2) and (y - (t + b) / 2) / ((t - b) / 2), by GDI's arctangent table (32 steps per 45 degrees, linear between) with its quadrant from the signs; the unit points by GDI's sine table (32 steps per 90 degrees, linear), or for a sweep in (0, 3) degrees by the series to the 12th power; one Bézier when both lie in one quadrant and the end's angle is past the start's, else a part quarter to the next axis, Ellipse's whole quarters (GDI.PATH.ELLIPSE) and a part quarter from the end's axis (a whole turn ends with a Bézier of no length); a part quarter's control points on the unit circle s + k (T - s), e + k (T - e) with T where the tangents at s and e cross and k = (4/3 c) / (c + 1), c the table cosine of half the span, mapped by the half axes and the middle and rounded to 28.4 halves away from 0; Arc, Pie and Chord begin a figure at the arc's start, ArcTo draws a line to it from the current position and moves it to the arc's end; Pie adds a line to the box's 28.4 middle and closes the figure, Chord closes it
GDI.PATH.ANGLEARC measured | AngleArc in a path; clean-room note on win32kfull (arcs); golden path anglearc-* | a line from the current position to the arc's start, then the circle's Béziers in 28.4 on the box (x - r, y - r) - (x + r, y + r) times 16 (no pixel in: the whole 2r): the start and end points by GDI's sine table (or its series for a sweep in (0, 3) degrees), each quadrant from the point's signs (an axis point: 0 at 0 degrees, 1 at 90, 2 at 180, 3 at 270); one Bézier for a sweep under 90 degrees whatever axes it crosses, else GDI.PATH.ARC's quarters, then for each whole turn past the first (at most 8) the turn back to the start and on to the end; a negative sweep mirrors the box top to bottom with the angles negated, the arc direction not asked; a start or end past 3600 degrees either way taken again from its point by the arctangent table; the current position moves to the arc's end
GDI.PATH.ROUNDFLAT measured | RoundRect in a path; golden path curve-158 arcpath-25 arcpath-48 arcpath-85 arcpath-90 | a corner of width or height 0 records Rectangle's figure (GDI.PATH.RECT); an empty box records nothing and succeeds unless a corner is 0 (GDI.PATH.CURVE)
GDI.PATH.ROUNDRECT measured | RoundRect in a path; golden path rrgrid-* arcpath-* curve-* (240 of 240 GetPath per arch) | the box (l, t) - (r - 1, b - 1) in 28.4 as Ellipse's; each corner's radius its width (height) times 8 in 28.4, shrunk as the box is, by (W - 1) / W, to the nearest (halves up), at most half the box; from the right side's upper end counterclockwise (with AD_CLOCKWISE the mirror: from its lower end), each corner a Bézier quarter whose control points are the radius times 1 - 4/3 (sqrt 2 - 1) in, rounded as Ellipse's (down across; down the box up counterclockwise, down clockwise), the sides lines, the figure closed
