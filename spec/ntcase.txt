# ntdll's case tables over every UTF-16 unit, settled by the golden ntcase answers (ntcase-32 and
# ntcase-64 agree with each other and with the model on all 1024 cases).
NT.CASE.UP measured | RtlUpcaseUnicodeChar | RtlUpcaseUnicodeChar maps a unit by one table of 973 one-to-one (lowercase, uppercase) pairs, a subset of Unicode's simple mappings (not U+00B5, U+0131, U+017F, the title-case digraphs, U+03C2, U+03F4 or later additions); every other unit is itself (golden ntcase-32/-64)
NT.CASE.DOWN measured | RtlDowncaseUnicodeChar | RtlDowncaseUnicodeChar maps a unit by the same table the other way (golden ntcase-32/-64)
NT.CASE.TOWUPPER measured | (none) | ntdll's own towupper is RtlUpcaseUnicodeChar on every unit (golden ntcase-32/-64; the clean-room notes: a tail jump)
NT.CASE.TOWLOWER measured | (none) | ntdll's own towlower adds 0x20 to 'A'..'Z' and U+00C0..U+00DE but U+00D7, every other unit unchanged (golden ntcase-32/-64; the clean-room notes)
