GDI.LINE.PIXELS assumed | (none) | a one-pixel line lights one pixel per step of its major axis (the longer of |dx|, |dy|; x when equal), the minor coordinate rounded from start + i * minor / major
GDI.LINE.TIE measured | (none) | a minor coordinate exactly halfway between two pixels goes to the smaller one (up for x-major lines, left for y-major), whichever way the line runs (golden lines-32/-64: every line from the center of a 17x17 picture to every point of it and back, 578 lines)
GDI.LINE.POLY doc | Polyline, PolylineTo | lines between consecutive points; Polyline neither uses nor changes the current position, PolylineTo starts at it and leaves it at the last point

GDI.LINE.BEZIER measured | PolyBezier, PolyBezierTo; golden lines poly-* (14 of 14 per arch) | each curve is flattened in 28.4 as in a path (GDI.PATH.FLATTENHFD, the bound-box limits of the flattener included) and its points joined by 28.4 cosmetic lines (GDI.LINE.GIQ); PolyBezierTo leaves the current position at the last point
GDI.LINE.GIQ measured | golden lines poly-* | a cosmetic line between 28.4 points lights the pixels whose diamond it crosses and does not end in: the open diamond |x - cx| + |y - cy| < 1/2 with its right and bottom corners (the corners decide ties: whole ends give GDI.LINE.PIXELS with GDI.LINE.TIE); StrokePath's flattened curves too (path curve-*); a diagonal running along diamond edges (x - y or x + y half a pixel off the centers) meets no open diamond and lights instead the pixels whose lower edge on its line (lower left for x - y, lower right for x + y), closed at both corners, it runs along, less the one whose such edge holds the end (xform diagline-*, 24 of 24; path curve-47)

GDI.RECT.INVERT doc | InvertRect | inverts the rectangle's pixels (a logical NOT of each value)
GDI.RECT.EMPTY measured | golden lines fillrect-*, framerect-*, invertrect-* (31 cases) | FillRect and InvertRect take the corners in either order and draw nothing for an empty rectangle, returning 1; FrameRect fails (0) when right < left or bottom < top, and with right == left or bottom == top still draws its edges (top row t, bottom row b - 1, ...)
