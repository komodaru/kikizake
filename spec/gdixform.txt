GDI.MAP.ROUND measured | golden xform (11520 LPtoDP coordinates per arch) | Lx * xVE / xWE and the offset xVO - xWO * xVE / xWE each rounded to 1/16 (28.4 fixed point, halves up), their sum rounded to an integer (halves up)
GDI.MAP.DPROUND open | golden xform | DPtoLP's rounding when an extent ratio is not an integer (not the inverse of LPtoDP's: 7612 of 7680 coordinates fit each term rounded halves away from zero, ties differ)
