GDI.GRAD.TRIROUND measured | golden blend gradtri-* (60 cases, 7268 channels) | GRADIENT_FILL_TRIANGLE, 32 and 24 bpp: red, green and blue vertex values above 0xff00 count as 0xff00 (alpha not); a pixel's channel is the barycentric mix of the vertices' at the pixel, rounded to 16 bits halves up, its high byte; rows run from the edges as Wine's dibdrv draws them (edges followed from their right end, truncated)
