# scalers
WICIMAGE.SCALE.NEAREST measured | WICBitmapInterpolationMode (NearestNeighbor: the one pixel the point falls in) | output pixel d takes source pixel ceil((d + 1) * source / output) - 1 (the one under its right or bottom edge, not its center), in the source's format (golden wicscale: 136 rows, every width 1..=8 to 1..=17, and the columns)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.FORMAT measured | (none) | the format a scaler works in and answers with: NearestNeighbor, Linear, Cubic, Fant: gray of 8 bits or less 8bppGray, 24bppBGR as it is, 32bppBGRA / 32bppRGBA / 8bppAlpha premultiplied (WIC.FROM.PREMULTIPLY) and answered as 32bppBGRA (WIC.TO.UNPREMULTIPLY: a scaled straight picture loses what premultiplying loses), every other format 32bppPBGRA; Fant: 16bppGray as 32bppGrayFloat, 64bppRGBA as 128bppPRGBAFloat; HighQualityCubic: 16bppGray, 48bppRGB, 64bppRGBA, 32bppRGBA, 8bppAlpha as they are (golden wicscale fmt-*: 15 formats, every mode)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.LINEAR measured | WICBitmapInterpolationMode (Linear: the 2x2 pixels about the point) | the output pixel's center in 16.16 fixed point (step = (source << 16) / output truncated, center = (2i + 1) * step / 2 - 0.5), not before the first pixel; weights of 8 bits (the fraction's high byte) between that pixel and the next, the last pixel alone; across, then down, each pass truncated to 8 bits (golden wicscale row-*/rrow-*/fine-*/col-*: 303 gray cases, all as Windows)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.CUBIC measured | WICBitmapInterpolationMode (Cubic: the 4x4 pixels about the point) | across: Catmull-Rom (a = -1/2) at the 16.16 center of SCALE.LINEAR (not limited at the edges), the fraction cut to 6 bits, the 4 weights rounded to 16 bits and the third taking what they miss of 1, taps outside the picture its edge pixels, the sum truncated to 8 bits (golden wicscale rrow-*/row-*/fine-*: 297 rows, all as Windows)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.CUBIC.VERTICAL open | (none) | down: every column of ramps comes out lower than the same rule across (col-cubic-*: 7 -> 3 of 0..6 gives 0 2 4)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.FANT measured | WICBitmapInterpolationMode (Fant: all the pixels that map to it) | Fant's resampling: each output pixel the average over its span of the source, which within a source pixel is the linear interpolation from that pixel's value at its start to the next pixel's value at its end; exact in both directions, rounded once, halves up (golden wicscale: 303 gray cases, all as Windows; floating point or rounding between the passes misses up to 5)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.HQCUBIC measured | WICBitmapInterpolationMode (HighQualityCubic: a kernel resized with the scale) | Catmull-Rom about the exact center ((i + 0.5) * source / output - 0.5), widened by source / output when shrinking, the weights normalized, taps outside the picture its edge pixels; both directions without rounding between them, rounded once (golden wicscale: 303 gray cases, all as Windows)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.WIDE open | (none) | the filters in the formats of more than 8 bits a channel (Fant's 32bppGrayFloat and 128bppPRGBAFloat, HighQualityCubic's 16-bit formats, 32bppRGBA and 8bppAlpha)
WICIMAGE.SCALE.SIZE open | IWICBitmapScaler::Initialize | a size of 0 or past 65535
